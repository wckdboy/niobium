the aesthetic

Pixel art as a grown-up medium. Whole pixels, nearest-neighbour everywhere, nothing blurred and nothing cute for its own sake. Every character is an adult with presence: original fantasy and cyborg artwork, rendered as 48×48 portraits in the app, with a small companion creature. The interface around them is quiet: pale lavender surfaces, clear hierarchy and purple pixel accents. The bots are the point of the app; the art gives each one a face, and never gets in the way of the work.

real work underneath

A Pixel runs on Hermes and on-device DSH: its own profile, role, memory, tools, skills, model and a persistent Bot Chat. Pixels discuss tasks in engine-owned group rooms and message teammates with attribution. Hermes and local DSH keep independent connections and history, together or individually. Companions stand for real delegated subtasks, with progress, corrections and a Stop control. Nothing on screen is decoration for a state the gateway does not have.

the deck

  • home. Gateway health, measured round trip and the Pixel roster.
  • pixels. Persistent chats, rooms and a workshop that reviews a new teammate before creation.
  • engines. Choose Hermes or background DSH on device. The same native roster, workshop and chats.
  • activity. Tool calls, approvals and the work in flight.
  • settings. Connections, tools, appearance and runtime details.

character with boundaries

Wit, style and a mischievous edge; every human character is 18+, and the product includes no nudity, no explicit acts and no minors. Each Pixel gets a detailed operator briefing, explicit tool access and a named creature with its own temperament. Connections require account authorization; a generated persona never grants credentials.
