the aesthetic

Pixel art as a grown-up medium. Whole pixels, nearest-neighbour everywhere, nothing blurred and nothing cute for its own sake. Every character is an adult with presence: fantasy and cyborg, drawn with restraint and finished by hand on a 48×48 grid, with a companion creature at 16×16. The interface around them is quiet: a near-black field, one signal colour, and no ornament that does not carry information. The bots are the point of the app; the art gives each one a face, and never gets in the way of the work.

real work underneath

A Pixel is a Hermes bot: its own profile, role, memory, tools, skills, model and a persistent Bot Chat. Pixels discuss a task in group rooms and message teammates with attribution. Companions stand for real delegated subtasks, with progress, corrections and a Stop control. Nothing on screen is decoration for a state the gateway does not have.

the deck

  • home. Gateway health, measured round trip and the Pixel roster.
  • pixels. Persistent chats, rooms and a workshop that reviews a new teammate before creation.
  • local. Alpine + iSH + DeepSeek Harness in the iOS full build; remote DSH on both platforms.
  • activity. Tool calls, approvals and the work in flight.
  • settings. Connections, tools, appearance and runtime details.

character with boundaries

Wit, style and a mischievous edge; every human character is 18+, and the product includes no nudity, no explicit acts and no minors. Each Pixel gets a detailed operator briefing, explicit tool access and a named creature with its own temperament. Connections require account authorization; a generated persona never grants credentials.
