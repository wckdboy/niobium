Describe a job. Review a teammate. The bot-build skill designs their voice, character, companion and access from the capabilities on your selected engine.

01 / describe

“A sharp-tongued night-shift security witch for my VPS logs, with a tiny three-eyed bat. Ask before restarting anything.”

The skill writes a 120–600 word operator briefing: expertise, working habits, a quirk, voice, approval boundaries, collaboration and delegation. A playful edge stays adult, opt-in and non-explicit.

02 / review

The preview shows the name, role, traits, briefing, adult age, 48×48 character recipe, named companion, tools, skills and requested connections. Network, shell and filesystem access can be changed before creation. No profile is created while the model is designing it.

If the model is unavailable or its response fails validation, the preview is clearly labelled as a local template. Unavailable and disallowed capabilities are excluded.

make it yours

Remix a look before creating the Pixel, or open Pixel studio later. Pin your favorite traits and shuffle the rest: build, colors, skin tone, hair color, hair style, expression, outfit, accessory and detail. Restore the authored cast whenever you like. Appearance edits preserve the Pixel’s access and briefing.

Both apps use exact 48×48 portraits and twelve independent 16×16 companions. Axolotl, imp, wisp and mimic join the original eight. Changing a companion keeps the character’s portrait.

Offline templates have eight distinct working voices, from a skeptical researcher to a mischievous editor. A connected model can design a custom persona through the bot-build skill. The preview labels which path was used. Custom recipes now use smaller adult faces, longer torsos, a relaxed asymmetric pose and two tailored outfit cuts. Bold strappy tops, cropped jackets, open coats and bare shoulders keep opaque coverage. Large custom previews blink gently; small roster portraits stay still and system reduced motion is respected. The authored cast and layered generator remain distinct art treatments.

03 / create

On Hermes, Niobium creates a profile, writes its briefing, pins reviewed toolsets and skills, disables inherited MCP servers, keeps command approvals manual, and saves the PixelSpec in profile metadata. On DSH, it writes a real preset from trusted plugin rows and stores the persona and appearance. Model credentials come from the selected engine. DSH skills share the local workspace; individual skill pins and native connector setup remain limited.

Connection requests do not authorize accounts. Workspace and network isolation are enforced on the Hermes host. If setup fails after creation, Niobium identifies the incomplete profile instead of creating another one silently.

04 / work together

Hermes Pixels collaborate in persistent DMs and group rooms. A companion helps with a bounded subtask through Hermes’ delegation tools. The parent remains responsible for checking and combining the results.
