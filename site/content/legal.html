privacy

The native clients connect to the Hermes or DSH endpoints you configure. Chat, files and tool requests go to those systems and their configured providers. Android gateway tokens use Keystore; desktop tokens remain in memory for the current app session. On the retained iOS client, tokens use Keychain; provider credentials remain on the configured harness or host. Local appearance choices and connection preferences are stored on the device.

Optional AI artwork sends the brief and reference image directly to OpenAI using the image key you supply. The app requests provider-side storage to resume background jobs. OpenAI’s usage and retention rules apply. The image key is session-only on desktop and uses Keychain or Keystore on the mobile clients; the saved job receipt and compact result stay on the device.

This static website uses self-hosted fonts and no client JavaScript or analytics. Hosting infrastructure may process ordinary HTTP access logs. Your gateway and providers have their own policies.

characters and assets

Every human Pixel is an adult, minimum age 18. Fantasy and cyborg styling may be mature and revealing; the product does not include nudity, explicit sexual acts or minors. Companions are original fictional creatures.

The new terrace and cast art were generated for this project. The procedural layer engine and companion sprites are Niobium assets. Legacy optional portrait files include CC0 artwork by Hyptosis; their source and provenance remain documented in the repository.
