what changed

The desktop workspace shares Kotlin transport, Pixel specifications, persona creation and image palette reduction with Android. Separate Hermes and local DSH workspaces provide persistent chats, streamed tools, models, recovered approvals/questions and engine-owned rooms.

32 adult fantasy/cyborg looks, eight garment palettes and transparent creatures carry over to the desktop wardrobe. A selected look opens directly in the workshop. Optional AI designs preserve receipts, reject unusable images and require an explicit request before another paid design.

Local DSH has private authentication, explicit Start/Stop, graceful task cleanup and adapter paths that survive moving the app. Installer checks cover the actual packaged launcher, runtime hashes and executable permissions. CodeRabbit now reviews new changes; Greptile automatic reviews are disabled.

verified

33 shared-core tests, 18 desktop tests, seven additional Android JVM tests, 67 Node checks and 15 Python checks. Sixty-four portable wardrobe fixtures verify exact pixel rendering. Android build, lint, runtime hashes, signing and 16 KB ELF/ZIP alignment pass.

All 16 ARM64 Android 16 emulator checks passed after the shared-core extraction; the two affected DSH/transport checks were rerun after the transport fixes. The signed APK installs and cold-launches. Native Linux and macOS CI exercises real DSH restart/relocation, app images and extracted or mounted installers.
