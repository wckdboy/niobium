verification

The beta passed 28 iOS unit tests, 3 native UI tests, 25 Android JVM tests and 5 app-domain runtime tests, 18 DSH adapter tests, 13 Hermes watcher tests, 5 asset checks and 38 Swift PixelSpec assertions, plus native builds, Android lint and site checks. Greptile review and CI cover the release changes. Runtime tests include a real native DSH preset/chat/history round trip through a controlled model server, native navigation with DSH in the background, authenticated HTTP/WebSocket access, Stop/restart and workspace preservation. The full iOS Debug acceptance app also passed a real DSH turn through a controlled SSE model, an eight-second Home-screen round trip and stored history after a cold app/guest launch. Its separate sandbox protects the installed app; Release omits the fixture hook. Fixture and demo tests do not replace real-account or physical-device acceptance.
