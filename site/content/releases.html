verification

The beta passed 33 iOS unit tests, four native UI tests, 29 Android JVM tests, eight Android runtime/renderer/editor checks, 18 DSH adapter tests, 13 Hermes watcher tests, seven asset/catalog checks and 38 Swift PixelSpec assertions, plus native release builds, Android lint, signatures and site checks. Portable seed fixtures verify matching recipes and exact RGBA pixels on Swift and Kotlin.

Runtime tests include a native DSH preset/chat/history round trip through a controlled model server, authenticated HTTP/WebSocket access, native navigation, Stop/restart and workspace preservation. The full iOS build 9 acceptance app passed a controlled SSE model turn, eight seconds on the Home screen and history after a cold app/guest restart in 104.198 seconds. Production Release omits the Debug fixture hook. Real-account and physical-device acceptance remain separate.

CI passes all three jobs. Greptile found two distinct issues, now fixed and regression-tested; its 50-credit trial limit prevented a final re-review. Independent Codex review of the final implementation found no further issues. Review and fixes → · Repeat the checks →
